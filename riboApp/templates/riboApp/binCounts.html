Gene Bin Counts Analysis

📊 What is Bin Counts Analysis?

Bin Counts Analysis examines how ribosome footprint reads are distributed across the coding sequence (CDS) of genes. This analysis helps identify patterns in ribosome density and translation efficiency.

🔬 How it works:

  • Step 1: Skip 45 nucleotides from the start codon and 15 nucleotides before the stop codon to avoid edge effects
  • Step 2: Divide the remaining CDS into 4 equal bins
  • Step 3: Count ribosome footprint reads in each bin
  • Step 4: Normalize counts to show the distribution pattern

📈 Interpretation:

  • Uniform distribution: Normal, efficient translation
  • Higher density in early bins: Possible ribosome queuing or slow initiation
  • Higher density in later bins: Potential ribosome stalling or pausing
  • Irregular patterns: May indicate regulatory elements or translation control
