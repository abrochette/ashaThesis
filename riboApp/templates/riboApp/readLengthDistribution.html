About Read Length Distribution Analysis

  • Normalized Plot (Top): Shows read length distributions normalized by total reads per sample, allowing direct comparison between samples with different sequencing depths.
  • Individual Plots: Show raw read counts for each sample, useful for understanding the absolute abundance of different read lengths.
  • Read Length: Measured in nucleotides (nt). Typical ribosome-protected fragments range from ~20-35 nt.
  • Quality Control: Consistent read length distributions across replicates indicate good experimental reproducibility.
