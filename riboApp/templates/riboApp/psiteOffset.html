About This Analysis:

This page helps you determine and configure P-site offsets for your ribosome profiling data. The plot shows read coverage around stop codons without any P-site shifts applied, allowing you to identify the optimal offset for each read length.

  • Stop Codon Reference: The red dashed line marks the stop codon position
  • No P-site Shifts: Raw read positions are shown to help determine proper offsets
  • Periodicity Pattern: Look for 3-nucleotide periodicity upstream of the stop codon
  • Offset Determination: The distance from the main peak to the stop codon indicates the required P-site offset
