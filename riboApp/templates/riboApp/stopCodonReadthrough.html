🧬 Stop Codon Readthrough Analysis

Analyze ribosome readthrough patterns separated by stop codon type (TAA, TAG, TGA). This analysis shows P-site coverage around stop codons, grouped by the specific stop codon sequence.

📊 About This Analysis

  • TAA (Ochre) - Typically shows lowest readthrough rates
  • TAG (Amber) - Shows intermediate readthrough rates
  • TGA (Opal) - Typically shows highest readthrough rates
  • Readthrough Region - Positive positions (right of stop codon) indicate continued translation
  • P-site Adjusted - Uses P-site offsets to accurately position ribosomes

💡 Interpretation Guide:

  • Position 0 - The stop codon location (red dashed line)
  • Negative positions - Upstream of stop codon (normal translation)
  • Positive positions - Downstream of stop codon (readthrough region, shaded gray)
