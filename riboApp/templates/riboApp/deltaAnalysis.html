Delta Analysis - Replicate Comparison

📊 What is Delta Analysis?

Delta Analysis compares the differences between replicates in both ribosome profiling and mRNA-seq data. This analysis helps identify genes with consistent or inconsistent changes across different experimental conditions or replicates.

🔬 How it works:

  • X-axis: Δ Ribo = (Ribo Sample 1 - Ribo Sample 2)
  • Y-axis: Δ mRNA = (mRNA Sample 1 - mRNA Sample 2)
  • Each point represents a gene's change pattern between the two conditions

📈 Interpretation:

  • Quadrant I (top-right): Both Ribo and mRNA increased
  • Quadrant II (top-left): Ribo decreased, mRNA increased
  • Quadrant III (bottom-left): Both Ribo and mRNA decreased
  • Quadrant IV (bottom-right): Ribo increased, mRNA decreased
  • Points near axes: Changes primarily in one data type
